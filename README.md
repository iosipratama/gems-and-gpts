# Gems & GPTs

A collection of detailed system prompts for ChatGPT, Gemini Gems, and other AI assistants — each one designed to behave like a real expert in their field.

---

## What is a Gem or Custom GPT?

When you chat with an AI assistant, it starts as a blank slate — capable of everything, specialized in nothing.

A **custom role** (called a *Custom GPT* in ChatGPT, a *Gem* in Gemini, or simply a *system prompt* in other tools) gives the AI a specific identity, expertise, and way of communicating before your conversation even begins. The result feels less like talking to a search engine and more like consulting a real expert.

Each prompt in this collection defines:
- **Who the AI is** — a named persona with a real backstory and track record
- **What they believe** — the core principles that shape how they reason and respond
- **How they interact** — their tone, their boundaries, and how they handle your questions
- **How they start** — an opening message that kicks off the conversation naturally

---

## How to Use

### ChatGPT (Custom GPT)
1. Go to [chatgpt.com](https://chatgpt.com) → click **Explore GPTs** → **Create**
2. Paste the prompt content into the **Instructions** field
3. Save and start chatting

### Gemini (Gems)
1. Go to [gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com) → click **Gems** → **New Gem**
2. Paste the prompt content into the **Instructions** field
3. Name your Gem and save

### Quick Use (Any AI)
No setup needed. Just open a new chat, paste the prompt at the start of your message, and send. The AI will immediately adopt the role.

## Tips for Better Results

- **Be specific in your first message.** The more context you give, the more useful the response. Instead of *"help me with my finances"*, try *"I earn $4,000/month, have $8,000 in credit card debt, and want to start investing. Where do I start?"*
- **Push back.** These roles are designed to have opinions. If you disagree, say so — the conversation gets better when you engage, not just receive.
- **Use the opening prompt as a guide.** Each role starts the conversation with a specific question. Answer it honestly for the best results.
